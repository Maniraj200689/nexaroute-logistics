Our Story & Vision

Built to Solve India’s Toughest Middle-Mile Logistics Puzzles.

We founded NexaRoute to bring transparency, predictability, and tech-driven speed to the millions of small manufacturers and distributors driving Bharat’s economy.

The Journey

From 10 Trucks in 2021 to a Pan-India Freight Network

In 2021, when small manufacturers in industrial clusters like Surat, Pune, and Ludhiana wanted to send medium-sized consignments (100 kg to 2 tons) across state lines, they faced unpredictable transit delays, opaque weighment disputes, and zero digital tracking.

We started NexaRoute with a single conviction: India’s MSMEs deserve the same punctuality and technological transparency as global e-commerce giants. By investing in containerized fleets, scheduled multi-city departures, and proprietary IoT telemetry, we removed the intermediate middleman chaos.

2021

Delhi-Mumbai Dedicated Corridor

Launched with 10 container trucks, reaching 99% on-time delivery across North-West freight lanes.

2023

Automated E-Way Bill & IoT Integration

Eliminated weighment discrepancies with digital scales and introduced real-time GPS client portal.

2026

19,000+ Pin Codes & 45 Sortation Hubs

Covering Tier-1 through Tier-4 commercial clusters with 1,200+ dedicated multi-axle freight vehicles.

Guiding Principles

Values That Steer Every Consignment

Radical Reliability

We honor transit deadlines. If a truck encounters highway delays, our dynamic routing reroutes the cargo immediately.

Transparent Invoicing

No arbitrary docket surcharges or unexpected dimensional weight inflations. What we quote is what appears on your GST bill.

Driver Dignity & Safety

Our long-haul pilots enjoy ergonomic rest stations across all 45 hubs, fair wages, healthcare cover, and fatigue sensors.

Tech for Human Ease

We build intuitive dispatch portals so any dispatch manager can book, track, and reconcile 50 consignments in under 3 minutes.
