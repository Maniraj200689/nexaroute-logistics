Six Specialized Services Built for Indian Commerce

Engineered to handle varied cargo types, SLAs, temperature tolerances, and multi-hub distribution requirements.

1. Express Part Truckload (PTL)

Ship cargo between 50 kg and 3,000 kg without paying for an entire vehicle. Scheduled departures leave daily on fixed line-haul schedules.

SLA: 24 to 72 hrs intercity transit
Best For: Textiles, electronics, auto spare parts
Bonus: Doorstep pickup and digital ePOD

2. Full Truckload (FTL) Pan-India

Dedicated containerized vehicles exclusively for your consignment. Point-to-point transit eliminates sorting dock risks and handling damages.

Fleet: 14ft, 20ft, 32ft MXL containers
Security: Smart GPS electronic lock & geo-fencing
Best For: Bulk industrial, FMCG, machinery

3. Warehousing & 3PL Fulfillment

Store inventory closer to your regional buyers in our Grade-A fulfillment hubs across Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Bengaluru, and Kolkata.

Features: WMS integration, pallet storage, barcoding
Operations: Pick, pack, kit, and dispatch within 4 hours
Best For: D2C brands, omnichannel distributors

4. Cold Chain & Pharma Freight

Refrigerated containers equipped with IoT temperature telemetry and live alerts for sensitive pharmaceuticals and perishable foodstuffs.

Temp Ranges: Chilled (2°C - 8°C) & Frozen (-18°C)
Assurance: Continuous data logger reports on delivery
Best For: Vaccines, biologics, dairy, frozen goods

5. Hyperlocal Urban B2B Dispatch

Same-day and 24-hour intra-city freight transfer connecting factory gates directly to wholesale dealer shops and retail points.

Speed: 4-6 hour guaranteed urban transit windows
Green Fleet: 35% electric commercial vehicles (EV)
Best For: Kirana store replenishment, spare parts

6. Reverse Logistics & Returns

Structured B2B reverse pickups for defective inventory, warranty refurbishments, and end-of-season retail inventory recalls.

Quality Check: On-spot barcode verification and QC tag
Reconciliation: Automated credit note matching
Best For: Consumer durables, fashion recalls
